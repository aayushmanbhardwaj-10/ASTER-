P1.2 Semantic Formula Contracts are frozen.

Decision summary:
- Multiple formula variants are supported.
- Fidelity ranking is ontology preference and evidence availability gates selection.
- Equal-rank variants require deterministic secondary precedence or explicit ambiguity.
- Temporal bindings are semantic relationships, not string offsets.
- FLOW/STOCK/POINT_IN_TIME behavior and aggregation requirements are explicit.
- Time-series rollups are conditional; standalone quarterly flows may be summed only when non-overlapping and context-compatible; YTD values are not blindly summed.
- Stock AS_OF_LATEST behavior is conditional on source and temporal semantics.
- Sign normalization is explicit, reversible, and provenance-preserving.
- Dimensional semantics are distinct from display units.
- Semantic expressions and structured expression trees are non-executable ontology metadata.
- Algebraic equivalence requires explicit solution variants.
- Constraints, assumptions, formula versions, ontology versions, and provenance remain identifiable.
- The LLM has no authority to select, rewrite, or execute financial formulas.
- The ontology never executes financial mathematics.
